Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need internet to use DAPDEK software?

No. Our software runs on your own phone or computers, so work continues when data or network is down. AlertGuard, POS Agent Day Book, AjoBook and Dues & Levies Manager keep your phone and laptop in sync over your own Wi-Fi. MediCore runs on your hospital network.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, every product has one. Hotel Manager has a 30-day free trial; the others have a 7-day free trial. No payment is needed to start.

What is the difference between yearly and lifetime?

A yearly licence is renewed every year. A lifetime licence is paid once and does not need renewal. Your licence key activates offline.

Does AlertGuard work on iPhone?

No. iPhone does not allow apps to read bank notifications, so AlertGuard needs an Android shop phone. It is proven with Moniepoint Business; ask us about your bank.

Does AjoBook or POS Agent Day Book hold or move money?

No. AjoBook keeps records only and never holds or moves money. POS Agent Day Book does not connect to your POS terminal; you record what happened and the app does the maths.

Where is my data kept?

Records stay on your own devices. You can back up any time and keep copies on a flash drive or your own cloud.

Can I bring in my existing lists?

Yes, where the product supports it. For example, MediCore imports patient lists from Excel or CSV. Tell us what you have and we will advise.

Can you build a custom application for my business?

Yes. Tell us how your business works and we will build an application for it, with your name and logo. It can work offline on your own phones and computers, online as a web app you open from anywhere, or both. Message us on WhatsApp to get started.

How do I buy or get help?

Chat with us on WhatsApp at +234 806 227 3394 or email dapdekglobalventures@gmail.com. We help by phone, WhatsApp and email, with picture-by-picture guides.
