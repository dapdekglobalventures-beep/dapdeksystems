Computer-based testing

Set an exam once and deliver it to a full computer lab, marked automatically with results the same afternoon. Runs over the local network when the internet drops, and syncs when it returns — built for terminal exams, mocks and entrance tests alike.

  • Question bank by subject
  • Timed exam sessions
  • Offline lab delivery
  • Automatic marking
  • Candidate registration
  • Result sheets and analytics

What you can hold us to

It keeps working when the network doesn't

Exams, front desks and dispensaries run on the local network and sync back when the connection returns. Power and bandwidth are planned for, not assumed.

Your data stays yours

Host on our cloud or on a server in your building — your choice, and it can change later. Backups run nightly and you can export everything at any time.

Support answers
